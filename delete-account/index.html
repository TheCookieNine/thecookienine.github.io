Delete your Sudokufeud account

Sudokufeud: Multiplayer Sudoku · by The Coding Cookie · Last updated: 29 October 2026

You can delete your Sudokufeud account and its data at any time, directly in the app or by email.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Sudokufeud and tap Profile on the main screen.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and the app starts over on a new guest account.

Delete it by email

If you no longer have the app, email thecodingcookie@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Sudokufeud account" and your username. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your player account and username, and the link to your Google, Apple or email sign-in.
  • Your game data: rating, wins, losses, win streaks, solved puzzles, best times and leaderboard entries.
  • Your hints, diamonds, themes, other items and daily rewards.
  • Your purchases in the game, including "No Ads". They can't be restored after the account is deleted.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies, and by us only where the law requires it (for example for accounting).
  • Anonymous usage statistics that can't be linked to you.

Everything else is permanently deleted and can't be recovered.

If you signed in with Apple, you can also remove Sudokufeud from your Apple ID under Settings → [your name] → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

See also our privacy policy.
